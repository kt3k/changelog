### **Iterator.prototype.includes is now specified** (837168a)
A new `Iterator.prototype.includes(searchElement[, skippedElements])` method has been added to the standard library. The algorithm handles iterator validation, optional skipping, range checks, and `SameValueZero` matching, making the proposal concrete in the spec.

### **Parsing and grammar sections are reorganized** (21e4774, 9ef1768, bdfe05b, 83df5c6, 7baa612, 3281a46)
The parsing material was split into a dedicated "Parsing" section and updated to more directly relate `ParseText` to parsing. Related grammar terminology was generalized and clarified, including runtime grammars, mnemonic terminal symbols, and colon usage, with a new example for multi-character syntactic-grammar terminals.

### **RegExp.prototype[%Symbol.split%] explanation tightened** (dbf6bc1, 43f12a7, cb86671)
The split-method text and embedded code formatting were brought into better alignment for consistency. The explanatory prose was also improved, which should make the behavior easier to follow.

### **Disposable-resource and binding assertion cleanups** (42b6e63, 06a7fa0, e3b9839)
`CreateDisposableResource` was inlined into `AddDisposableResource`, and a few calls were updated to use `!` where the spec now guarantees they cannot throw. These are readability and notation cleanups, not behavioral changes.

### **Other misc changes**
- Introduced the term "non-zero finite Number".
- Removed an incorrect `[[NoTear]]` note.
