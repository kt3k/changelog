### **Number terminology is normalized across the spec** (2fda459)
The spec replaces the overloaded term “finite” with “finite Number” in core Number definitions and algorithms, and adds a dedicated autolink target for that concept. This is mostly editorial, but it removes ambiguous wording and fixes incorrect linking in multiple clauses.

### **IsLooselyEqual now states the BigInt/Number finite-check directly** (2477796)
The loose-equality algorithm now folds the finite-number requirement into the BigInt-vs-Number branch itself. That makes the precondition clearer and keeps the comparison logic aligned with the surrounding spec language.

### **Time values gain an explicit “finite time value” term** (214a2d9)
The time-values section now names the integral, non-NaN case as a “finite time value.” This gives later references a precise term to point at without changing the underlying time model.

### Other misc changes
- Number.prototype math/formatting algorithms were editorially aligned around finite-number preconditions and RangeError wording.
- Number.isFinite and Number.MAX_VALUE wording was simplified for consistency.
- Number::remainder assertions were split for readability.
- Additional editorial cleanup in numeric comparison and power/remainder clauses.
