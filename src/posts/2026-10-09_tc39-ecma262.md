### **TypedArray revalidation now checks the whole element** (5e0f8b3)
`RevalidateAtomicAccess` now computes the last byte of the accessed element and compares that against the cached buffer length, rather than checking only the starting byte. That closes a gap for multi-byte TypedArray elements and matters for correctness on growable SharedArrayBuffers.

### **Numeric literal grammar was simplified** (874aedc, 78ea84c)
The numeric-literal productions were tightened up by folding the `0`/`NonZeroDigit` alternatives into `DecimalDigit` and simplifying `NonOctalDecimalIntegerLiteral` around a shared `LegacyOctalLikeDecimalDigits` helper. This mostly reduces grammar duplication and makes the legacy-octal-vs-decimal distinction clearer.

### **Specification examples were normalized** (5345883, 53a7267, 3be9c1c)
Several explanatory examples and MV clauses were edited for consistency: the “one of” example was made smaller, and prose like “plus”/“times” was standardized to symbolic `+`/`×`. These are readability-only changes and do not alter semantics.

### Other misc changes
- Editorial MV wording cleanup across numeric and escape-sequence clauses
- Reference/oldid update for the renamed numeric-literal production
