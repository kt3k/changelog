### Typed array algorithms now use element-specific helpers
**TypedArray indexed access tightened** — Several abstract operations now call `TypedArrayGetElement` and `TypedArraySetElement` instead of generic `Get`/`Set` on stringified indices, making the spec’s typed array semantics more explicit and precise.

### Spec wording and naming cleanup
**Internal aliases standardized** — A small editorial sweep replaced some terse internal variable names with clearer forms like `_value_` and `_name_`, and renamed a configurable-flag helper for consistency.

### Other misc changes
**Date/time wording fix** — “timezone” was corrected to “time zone” in date/time formatting text.
