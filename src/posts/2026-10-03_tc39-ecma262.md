### **Iterator.prototype.join lands in the spec** (6db9135)
ECMA-262 now defines `Iterator.prototype.join(separator)`, which consumes an iterator, stringifies its values, and concatenates them with a separator (defaulting to `,`).

It also spells out iterator-safe abrupt completion handling and skips `undefined`/`null` values during concatenation, making the new API's behavior precise for implementers.
