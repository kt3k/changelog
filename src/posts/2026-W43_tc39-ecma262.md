### **Iterator helpers grow new grouping primitives**
ECMA-262 adds `Iterator.prototype.chunks(_chunkSize_)` and `Iterator.prototype.windows(_windowSize_, _undersized_)`, giving iterators built-in fixed-size grouping and sliding-window behavior. The new methods validate sizes, close on abrupt completion, and yield arrays for each group.

### **List-joining behavior is centralized**
`Array.prototype.join`, `Array.prototype.toLocaleString`, and `%TypedArray%.prototype.toLocaleString` now share a common `JoinIndexedProperties` abstract operation. The refactor also broadens the helper for ECMA-402 use, adds method-argument support, and factors out `LocalizedListSeparator()` for locale-sensitive separators.

### **Spec terminology and abstract ops get cleanup**
Several low-level editorial updates landed, including renaming the default `validElementTypes` value in `CreateListFromArrayLike` from `~all~` to `~any~`, plus broader wording cleanup around bitwise AND/XOR/OR references and consolidation around `BigIntBitwiseOp`.

### Other misc changes
- Spell-check script now emits UTF-8 dictionary output.
- Minor editorial cleanup around `%TypedArray%.prototype.toLocaleString`, `JoinIndexedProperties`, and related esmeta ignores.
