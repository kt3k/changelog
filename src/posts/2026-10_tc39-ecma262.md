### New iterator helpers and methods
**Iterator.prototype.join, chunks, windows, and includes** — The month added several iterator helpers to ECMA-262: `join()` for separator-based string concatenation, `chunks()` for fixed-size grouping, `windows()` for sliding windows, and `includes()` for `SameValueZero` search with optional skipped elements. These algorithms also spell out iterator closing and abrupt-completion behavior.

### Typed arrays and DataView get correctness fixes
**TypedArray revalidation and method semantics tightened** — `RevalidateAtomicAccess` now checks the full accessed element instead of just the starting byte, closing a gap for multi-byte elements on growable SharedArrayBuffers. Separately, `filter` and `with` were corrected to account for detachment/shrinking during callbacks, and the spec now clarifies the observable `undefined` cases and suppresses unintended user-code effects.

**DataView accessors no longer throw on detached buffers** — `DataView.byteLength` and `DataView.byteOffset` now return `+0` rather than throwing `TypeError` when the buffer is detached or out of bounds, removing a surprising exception path and aligning with the updated normative text.

### Parsing, grammar, and shared abstract operations were reorganized
**Parsing section split out and grammar language clarified** — The parsing material was reorganized into a dedicated section, with `ParseText` more directly tied to parsing. Related grammar terminology was cleaned up and generalized, including runtime grammars, mnemonic terminals, and punctuation examples.

**List-joining logic was centralized** — `Array.prototype.join`, `Array.prototype.toLocaleString`, and `%TypedArray%.prototype.toLocaleString` now use a shared `JoinIndexedProperties` abstract operation, which was also generalized for ECMA-402 use and paired with a dedicated `LocalizedListSeparator()` helper.

### Spec terminology and editorial cleanup
**Number and time terminology was normalized** — The spec standardized on terms like “finite Number,” “finite time value,” and “non-zero finite Number,” and updated related numeric algorithms for clearer preconditions and consistent linking.

**Various algorithm and prose cleanups** — The month also included realm-sensitive prototype fallback clarification, bitwise-operation terminology unification, a `CreateListFromArrayLike` default rename, tighter `RegExp.prototype[%Symbol.split%]` prose, and a few readability-only refactors in disposable-resource and numeric clauses.

### Other misc changes
- Numeric-literal grammar was simplified and examples were normalized.
- An IPR check was updated for a renamed author account.
- PR preview deployments were adjusted to externalize TOCs and avoid race-related deploy loss.
