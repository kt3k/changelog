### TypedArray correctness
**RevalidateAtomicAccess now checks the full element** — The revalidation logic was updated to compare the last byte of an accessed element against the cached buffer length, not just the starting byte. This closes a correctness gap for multi-byte TypedArray elements, especially with growable SharedArrayBuffers.

### Grammar simplification
**Numeric literal productions were tightened up** — The decimal and legacy-octal-related grammar was refactored to reduce duplication, folding the `0`/`NonZeroDigit` alternatives into `DecimalDigit` and simplifying `NonOctalDecimalIntegerLiteral` around a shared helper. This is mainly a cleanup that makes the spec structure easier to follow.

### Editorial cleanup
**Examples and MV wording were normalized** — Several explanatory examples and mathematical value clauses were edited for consistency, including smaller “one of” examples and standardized symbolic notation like `+` and `×`.

### Other misc changes
- Additional wording cleanup across numeric and escape-sequence clauses
- Reference/oldid updates for renamed numeric-literal productions
