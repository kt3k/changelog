### **Fix typed array assertions in `filter` and `with` (4feb027)**
The spec now explicitly notes that values can become *undefined* if user code detaches or shrinks the backing buffer during callback/coercion, and it marks the relevant `TypedArraySetElement` steps as suppressing user-code effects. This corrects an overly strong assertion and tightens the algorithms to match real observable behavior.
