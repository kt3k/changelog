### **Failed publishes now transition out of pending** (ed7c94a)
The publish handler now explicitly marks a task as failed if tarball upload or enqueueing fails, instead of leaving it in `pending` forever. That unblocks retries for the same version and prevents the CLI from getting stuck polling a task no worker will ever pick up.

### Other misc changes
- Stale-task reaper now considers `pending` publishing tasks too.
- Publish flow refactors and related DB/query/test updates to support the retry fix.
