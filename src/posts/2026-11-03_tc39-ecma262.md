### **Clarify DisposableStack.move() creation semantics** (24620e6)
The `{Async,}DisposableStack.prototype.move()` algorithms now use `OrdinaryObjectCreate` directly instead of `OrdinaryCreateFromConstructor`. This aligns the spec with the fact that these prototypes are non-writable and non-configurable, so the constructor fallback is never relevant.

### **Introduce descriptive figure references** (1c5d5e1)
The object/prototype and generator relationship figures were given more descriptive IDs and alt text, and nearby prose now xrefs them explicitly. This is an editorial cleanup that improves spec readability and makes the diagrams easier to reference consistently.
